Engineered for mission-critical enterprise autonomy

Every layer of retrieval, scoring, and generation is self-contained and auditable on your own server.

100% On-Premise Sovereign Data Guarantee

All embeddings, document chunks, PostgreSQL vectors, and local generative models run exclusively within your perimeter. No customer documents, confidential employee agreements, or proprietary schematics ever touch third-party cloud APIs.

Structural Grounded-Refusal Gate

A mathematical gate halts the prompt before it ever reaches the LLM if the top re-ranked chunk score is below threshold (-9.50). The system answers with an honest refusal rather than fabricating hallucinated facts.

Dense & Lexical Reciprocal Rank Fusion

Dense cosine vectors excel at conceptual meaning, while PostgreSQL BM25 full-text catches exact part numbers, codes, and names. RRF mathematically unites both candidate sets.

Multimodal Vision OCR Ingestion Pipeline

Incoming PDF files are pre-scanned with PyMuPDF to detect scanned pages, embedded figures, and financial tables. Plain text is indexed in milliseconds, while complex scanned figures route to GLM-OCR vision models.
